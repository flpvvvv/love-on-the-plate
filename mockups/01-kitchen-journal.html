The Kitchen Journal

Honest assessment

Good at

  • Twelve dishes read as a body of work: numbered entries, running count, month chapters.
  • Bilingual copy becomes the layout — 中文 headline over mono English annotation needs no language switch.
  • Quiet, low-chrome surfaces let the food and the writing carry the gallery.

Costs

  • Dense serif body needs 16 px+ and honest line-height or it tires in long descriptions.
  • Hairline-only structure gives up the tap-target affordance a card shadow provides.
  • Playfair has no CJK glyphs, so Chinese display type inherits PingFang — the two scripts never truly match in weight.

How to read this file

Every screen block below the toolbar is the same app, cloned from one template and initialised through data-* attributes only. Layout responds to the frame’s own width through container queries — no media queries are used for the app. The toolbar resizes every frame; Compare shows the phone and desktop frames together.
