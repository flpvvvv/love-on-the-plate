Direction C Neo-brutalism Bento + scoreboard Space Grotesk / Space Mono

Plate Stack

A fridge door covered in stickers, ordered like a grid: flat saturated blocks, 2.5 px ink rules, hard 6 px offsets, the gallery as a bento board and the numbers as a scoreboard — nothing soft, nothing rounded, everything stuck on with intent.

Tokens

  • --bw: 2.5pxevery border, both themes
  • --off: 6pxhard shadow offset, 0 0 0 blur
  • --shlight: --ink · dark: --cobalt block
  • radius: 0no rounded corner anywhere
  • presstranslate(6px,6px) + shadow 0; chips use 2px/2px
  • tapgrid tiles scale .97 (they cannot leave the grid)
  • motion140ms cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1) — snappy, no long eases
  • containers620 / 700 / 760 / 900 / 1080 px on .frame

Good at

  • A gallery that reads as a physical object — every plate is a sticker you can press, and the bento board gives the hero dish real weight without a carousel.
  • Numbers land instantly: mono numerals on cobalt, butter and tomato blocks turn analytics into a scoreboard instead of a dashboard.
  • Hierarchy with no gradients, no blur and no shadows to maintain — flat fills plus a 2.5 px rule survive any display, any brightness, and stay readable at 3 G.

Costs

  • Loud by construction. Cream is the only quiet surface, so long reading (both descriptions in the detail) needs generous spacing to stay calm.
  • Tomato and butter are unusable as text — three tokens exist purely as fills and every new component has to remember which is which.
  • Hard edges and uppercase everywhere cost warmth; the bilingual copy and the handwritten-feeling With love ♡ are what keep it a love letter rather than a poster.

1 · Gallery — Plates

The hero surface is a bento board laid on graph paper: one 2×2 hero sticker, then 1×1 and 2×1 tiles, every seam a 2.5 px ink rule. Below 900 px of container it is a two-column board with the bottom tab bar; above it the sidebar takes over and the board goes four wide.

2 · Gallery — Grid

Masonry as a sticker sheet: mixed portrait / landscape / square thumbs, two columns at phone width, four at desktop, each card pressed into its own 6 px block. The tail badge is the plate number, in Space Mono.

3 · Gallery — Timeline

Months are butter-yellow labels with a mono month number; rows stack under them at phone width and split either side of an ink centre rail at desktop, dots punched onto the rail.

4 · Immersive feed

Mobile only, full-bleed, scroll-snap, caption block welded to the bottom edge and the index stamped top-right as 3 / 12. Desktop has no Feed tab at all — press Desktop 1440 in the toolbar to watch this same panel turn into a horizontal filmstrip with a rail.

5 · Photo detail

Frame A is the phone bottom sheet: 4:3 plate, names, ingredient chips, long date, 中文 then a hairline then English, signed With love ♡, with prev/next and a swipe hint. Frame B is the desktop dialog with the delete confirmation open on top — its real copy, no edit or share actions anywhere.

6 · Search — expanded

Expanded search with the four field filters and the date presets, showing the 番茄 result set: the noodle soup, the mapo tofu and the tomato ingredient tag, counted in mono. Type in any other screen's search box to watch the cards themselves filter.

7 · Insights / Analytics

The scoreboard: total count and 30-day uploads as huge Space Mono numerals on tomato and cobalt blocks, then the eight-month bar chart, the ranked top dishes / top ingredients (toggle at phone width, both columns at desktop) and the 14-day trend.

8 · Upload (admin)

Frame A holds the drag state — the zone turns butter with ink on top and reads Drop your photo here. Frame B holds the finished AI review: editable dish name, EXIF date chip, editable ingredient chips, both descriptions and the real button set.

9 · Login

No app chrome, just the sticker card: magic-link form on the left, the sent state on the right with the address it was posted to.

10 · Component sheet

Every part laid out flat: buttons and their pressed, focused and disabled states, the dish text input, ingredient chips default and removable, the three-state view switcher, all three real toasts, skeleton and loaded and empty cards, the scroll-to-top button, the offline banner, the pull-to-refresh spinner and the theme toggle.
