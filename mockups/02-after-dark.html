What it is good at

  • Photography leads. The immersive feed is the home surface, not a tab — one plate per viewport, caption on glass, chrome pushed to hairline and blur.
  • One accent, spent carefully. Gold marks the active series, the active tab dot and the focus ring; nothing else competes with the food.
  • Evening grain. Near-black canvas with 17:1 ink reads as dinner service and makes low-light food photography look intentional.

What it costs

  • Light theme is a second design. “Daylight Service” needs its own luminance steps and a darkened gold; the dark palette cannot simply be inverted.
  • Dark-only photography risk. Bright, high-key plates can glare against the void, and the mosaic is deliberately quiet until hover — on touch it stays quiet.
  • Snap scrolling is a commitment. One plate per screen slows browsing down to a deliberate pace, and it needs a real fallback at desktop widths.

Gold on light — the correction. The raw accent #E0A85C measures 1.92:1 on #F7F3EE and 2.12:1 on white: it fails as text by a wide margin. In the light theme every gold glyph, icon, label and numeral therefore switches to #8A5A16 (5.35:1 on the canvas, 5.91:1 on white), exposed as a single swap of the --gold token. The raw #E0A85C survives only as --gold-soft — the rim light on a hovered mosaic tile and the illustration gradient — where it is a decorative fill, never a glyph.

Replaced from the current app: the cream “Morning Kitchen” canvas and the candlelit #0F0C0A dark, the --love #D94B38 / #FF7B68 red as the primary accent (now ember, reserved for focus and destructive only), --warmth amber (folded into gold), Fraunces, Plus Jakarta Sans and Caveat (now Playfair Display SC and Karla), and the card shadow stack (now a hairline plus one luminance step). Kept as-is: every screen, every route, the four gallery views, the search field set, the magic-link login, the AI review form and all of the app’s copy.

Preview

1 · Gallery — Plates

Hero surface. One plate per card on a luminance step, no border, no shadow — hover only lifts it 2 px. At a wide container the same panel becomes the full-bleed hero plate with a metadata rail and a horizontal filmstrip of the rest of the kitchen.

2 · Gallery — Grid

Quiet mosaic. A dense masonry at 72 % opacity and reduced saturation; a 300 ms warm rim-light wakes the tile you point at. On touch it stays quiet — the grid is for scanning, the feed is for looking.

3 · Gallery — Timeline

Dinner service, month by month. A gold month numeral over hairline rows at a narrow container; at a wide container the rows break onto an alternating centre rail with the plate sitting on the line.

4 · Immersive feed

The primary home surface, not a tab. One plate per viewport, scroll-snap-type: y mandatory, caption on a bottom scrim, tabular index 3 / 12 and a 1 px gold progress rail down the right edge. Mobile-only component: at a wide container the desktop home is the hero plate plus metadata rail and filmstrip instead. Arrow keys move the feed.

5 · Photo detail

Glass drawer. Rises from the bottom edge with a 40 px drag handle and an edge-to-edge image with no top radius; at a wide container it becomes a right-hand dialog. Metadata lands in a two-column caps grid — date · dimensions · size · filename. The third frame is the delete-confirmation variant, shown in the same glass language.

6 · Search — expanded

One field, four ways in. All / Title / Date / Tags, each with its own placeholder, plus date presets and an ingredient index. This frame is seeded with the fixture query 番茄 — the results line reads 3 results for 番茄 and the ingredient row 番茄 tomato carries the highlight. Type in the field to filter the twelve plates live.

7 · Insights / Analytics

Near-monochrome, gold only where it counts. Big serif numerals, bars on a luminance step, and the 14-day trend drawn as one glowing gold stroke over a gradient area. The two extra frames are the empty and the error states.

8 · Upload (admin)

Zone to review in one pass. Idle, dragging, uploading with per-file progress, then the AI review panel with every field editable, followed by recent uploads and the backfill card. The two extra frames put the dragging and the generated-result states side by side.

9 · Login

Magic link, no chrome. The form and the “Check your email” confirmation share one card set, stacked at a narrow container and side by side at a wide one.

10 · Component sheet

Every atom in the direction’s skin. Buttons in all four weights and their states, the dish field, ingredient chips, the three view-switcher positions, both toasts, the skeleton and the loaded/empty card, scroll-to-top, the network banner, the pull-to-refresh indicator and the theme toggle.
